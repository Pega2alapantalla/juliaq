About me

Hello! I'm Julia Quintana, an illustrator, graphic designer, and concept artist based in Cádiz, Spain.

Over the past few years, I have worked on illustration, graphic design, and visual communication projects. I led the Marketing and Graphic Design department for UCAnFly, a nanosatellite project at the University of Cádiz selected by the European Space Agency's Fly Your Satellite! programme. In this role, I developed the project's visual identity, designed graphic materials, managed social media, and created audiovisual content. Alongside this, I have worked as a freelance creative, producing illustrations, branding, and 3D visualizations for a variety of clients.

I consider myself a naturally curious person. I enjoy understanding how things work, asking questions, and finding creative solutions. I love collaborating with others, communicating ideas, and bringing a thoughtful and practical perspective to every project. Creativity is at the heart of everything I do, and I'm always looking for new ways to learn, grow, and improve my craft.

Exploration & Mediums

Throughout my career, I have explored a wide range of creative disciplines, including illustration, graphic design, sculpture, printmaking, photography, and digital art. I believe that experimenting with different techniques is one of the best ways to grow as an artist and discover new ways of telling stories. Rather than limiting myself to a single discipline, I enjoy taking on new challenges and learning tools that expand my creative process.

My work is inspired by imagination, nostalgia, and the unique way children perceive the world before rules reshape their perspective. I'm passionate about creating images that evoke emotion, spark curiosity, and tell stories that connect with people.

"To me, creativity is more than just a tool—it's a way of understanding the world."
