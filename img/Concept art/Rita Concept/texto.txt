Nombre: Rita Concept
Filtro: Concept art
Fecha: 15/06/2023
Descripción: An assignment developed for the Art and Technology course during my Fine Arts degree. The project focused on creating a full concept pipeline for a fictional production.
The overall project was based on a shared universe set in an apartment building, where each student designed a different floor and its inhabitant. This piece presents my contribution.

Rita is a 9-year-old only child living in the building. Forced to be a girl scout, she goes door to door selling stale cookies to her neighbors. Mischievous and observant, she spends her nights causing trouble—ringing doorbells at odd hours—while quietly watching everything that happens around her.
