Nombre: Bauhaus 90th Anniversary – Chair Design
Filtro: Graphic design personal
Fecha: 2023
Descripción: Commemorative design for the 90th anniversary of the Bauhaus. The piece features a composition of iconic Bauhaus chairs—Barcelona Chair, Weissenhof Chair, and Wassily Chair—arranged as a geometric motif. It explores form, structure, and color, reflecting the relationship between design, space, and abstraction central to the Bauhaus philosophy.
